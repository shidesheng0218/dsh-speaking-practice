# 中文母语者高频英语错误 20 条（B1）

| 错误例句（英文） | 更自然的说法（英文） | 中文解释（1 句） | 类型 |
|---|---|---|---|
| I very like this movie. | I really like this movie. | “很”不能直接修饰动词，用 really。 | 词性 |
| I am agree with you. | I agree with you. | agree 本身是动词，前面不用 be。 | 词性 |
| I have went to Beijing last year. | I went to Beijing last year. | 有明确过去时间用一般过去时。 | 时态 |
| I didn't went to the party. | I didn't go to the party. | didn't 后面接动词原形。 | 时态 |
| She is engineer. | She is an engineer. | 单数职业名词前要加 a 或 an。 | 冠词 |
| I like the music. | I like music. | 泛指一类事物时不用 the。 | 冠词 |
| I go to work by the bus. | I take the bus to work. | 交通方式用 by bus，不加 the。 | 冠词 |
| I have a lot of homeworks. | I have a lot of homework. | homework 不可数，没有复数。 | 单复数 |
| Can you give me some advices? | Can you give me some advice? | advice 不可数，不能加 -s。 | 单复数 |
| I'm looking forward to meet you. | I'm looking forward to meeting you. | to 是介词，后面接动名词。 | 搭配 |
| We discussed about the plan. | We discussed the plan. | discuss 是及物动词，不加 about。 | 搭配 |
| I'm interested about history. | I'm interested in history. | 固定搭配是 interested in。 | 介词 |
| I will arrive to Shanghai tomorrow. | I will arrive in Shanghai tomorrow. | 大地点前用 arrive in。 | 介词 |
| I have 25 years old. | I'm 25 years old. | 年龄用 be 动词，不用 have。 | 词性 |
| My English is very poor. | My English isn't great yet, but it's getting better. | 中文谦辞直译显消极，用 getting better。 | 中式表达 |
| I like travel. | I like travelling. | like 后接动名词或名词，不接动词原形。 | 词性 |
| He suggested me to try again. | He advised me to try again. | suggest 不接 sb to do，用 advise。 | 搭配 |
| I'm boring in this class. | I'm bored in this class. | boring 修饰物，bored 描述人的感受。 | 词性 |
| There have many people in the park. | There are many people in the park. | there be 句型表示“有”，不用 have。 | 中式表达 |
| This is my first time to visit Beijing. | This is my first time visiting Beijing. | first time 后接动名词更自然。 | 搭配 |
